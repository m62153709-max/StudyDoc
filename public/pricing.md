# Pricing — StudyDoc

## Free
- Price: $0/month (forever)
- PDF uploads: 3 AI-powered uploads per day
- Library: Saved paper library (synced to account)
- Explanations: Beginner and Intermediate levels
- Features: Section summaries, key takeaways, quiz mode, citations, concept diagrams
- Semantic search: No
- Tutor Mode: Limited

## Pro
- Price: $8/month
- PDF uploads: Higher daily limits
- Library: Full saved library with semantic search
- Explanations: Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert levels
- Features: Everything in Free, plus full Tutor Mode, paper comparison, priority processing, early access to new features
- Semantic search: Yes — search across your entire library by meaning
- Tutor Mode: Full — unlimited questions per paper

## Notes
- No credit card required for Free plan
- Cancel Pro anytime
- Both plans include OCR support for scanned PDFs
- Both plans include APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, and BibTeX citation generation
